Social media is built for the scroll, not the story. Boss Reminisce needed somewhere
his music and his journey could be found in full, not chopped into fifteen-second
pieces and gone by the next post.

His catalogue now lives at the centre of that home, front and centre on the site
itself, so listeners can move through his music the way it was meant to be heard,
start to finish, in one place that is entirely his.
