  challenge: >
    Every piece Mendy makes is knitted by hand, one stitch at a time, but that patience
    and care was not showing up anywhere online. The brand had no visual language of its
    own yet, and what did exist felt busy and ordinary, nothing like the quiet, personal
    craft behind each design. Mendy wanted people to feel the effort in her work before
    they even touched it, and to sense a maker behind the shop rather than a storefront.
  approach: >
    We are building the brand from the ground up around restraint. Clean lines, generous
    space, and a quiet colour language, so nothing will compete with the pieces
    themselves. Every layout, every product photo, every word is being chosen to feel
    considered rather than busy, giving the shop the same unhurried elegance as a
    handmade piece. We are also bringing her shop to life in real time, building in a
    live shopping feature so customers can watch new pieces debut and feel part of the
    moment, not just the transaction.
  outcome: >
    Mendys is nearly ready to open its doors, and it already looks and feels like the
    work that goes into it: personal, deliberate, quietly beautiful. Every piece of the
    brand is in place, waiting for the moment it meets its first customer. When the
    doors do open, shoppers will step into something that feels handmade before they
    have touched a single stitch, and the live drops will turn that first visit into an
    occasion worth showing up for.

Every piece Mendy knits carries hours of quiet, careful work, and the brand is being
built to hold that same patience. So we are letting restraint do the talking, giving
each design room to be seen properly, without noise competing for attention.

The live shopping feature will turn a browse into a moment. When Mendys opens, customers
will arrive not just to shop, but to watch something new come into the world, stitch by
stitch, the same way Mendy makes it.
