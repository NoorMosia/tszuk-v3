  challenge: >
    Mendys had great products and a loyal following, but the shop itself felt like a

    We treated the shop like a place, not a page. Every step, from the first look to the

    endure.
  outcome: >

Some shops sell to you. This one welcomes you. We were less interested in pushing
products and more interested in making people feel at ease enough to explore.

Every detail was shaped around the pleasure of finding something you did not know you
wanted, so that shopping Mendys feels less like a task and more like a treat.
